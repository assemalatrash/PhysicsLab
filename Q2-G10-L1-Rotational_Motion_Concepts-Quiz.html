💡 Lesson Hints & Formulas

1. Linear vs. Rotational Motion Twins

Linear Motion Rotational Twin SI Unit
Displacement (d) Angular Displacement (θ) rad
Velocity (v) Angular Velocity (ω) rad/s
Acceleration (a) Angular Acceleration (α) rad/s2

2. Core Rotational Formulas

  • Arc Length: s = r · θ
  • Average Angular Velocity: ω = θt
  • Linear (Tangential) Speed: v = r · ω
  • Angular Acceleration: α = ωf − ωit
  • Final Angular Velocity: ωf = ωi + α · t
  • Angular Displacement from Rest (ωi = 0): θ = 12 · α · t2

3. Key Concepts & Golden Rules

  • Golden Rule: Every point on a rigid spinning wheel or carousel has the exact same angular velocity (ω).
  • Radius Effect: Points farther from the center travel a bigger circle in the same time, so linear speed increases proportionally with radius (v = r · ω).
  • Steady Spin: If an object spins at a steady, unchanging angular velocity, its angular acceleration is α = 0 (rad/s2).
  • Slowing Down: When a spinning wheel slows down (ωf < ωi), its angular acceleration (α) is negative.

4. The 3-Line Math Rule Example

Finding θ from rest when α = 4 rad/s2 and t = 3 s:

Line 1 (Formula): θ = 12 · α · t2
Line 2 (Substitution): θ = 12 · 4 · 32
Line 3 (Answer): θ = 18 (rad)
