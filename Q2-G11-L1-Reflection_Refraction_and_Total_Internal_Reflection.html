Student-Friendly Optics Toolkit

OpenStax College Physics • Ch. 25 (25.2–25.4)

1. Law of Reflection

θᵢ = θᵣ
Golden Rule Warning: Always measure angles from the Normal Line (90°) — never from the mirror surface!

2. Index of Refraction

n = c / v   and   v = c / n
Speed of light in vacuum/air: c = 300,000 (km/s).
n is unitless (n ≥ 1). Higher n = Slower light!

3. Snell's Law (Refraction)

n₁ · sin(θ₁) = n₂ · sin(θ₂)
• Fast → Slow (n₁ < n₂): Slows down & bends TOWARD normal.
• Slow → Fast (n₁ > n₂): Speeds up & bends AWAY from normal.

4. Critical Angle & Total Internal Reflection

sin(θc) = n₂ / n₁
Hard Rule: TIR happens ONLY when n₁ > n₂ (slow → fast).
At θ₁ = θc, refracted ray skims the surface (θ₂ = 90°).
If θ₁ > θc, 100% of the light reflects back inside!

5. Magic Angle Values (Clean Math)

sin(30°) = 1/2  |  sin(90°) = 1
3-4-5 Triangle: sin(θ) = 3/5 or 4/5
n_air = 1

6. The 3-Line Method (Sample)

Speed of light in a crystal with n = 2:
1. Law: v = c / n
2. Sub: v = 300,000 / 2
3. Ans: v = 150,000 (km/s)
