💡 Lesson Hints & Formulas — Free Body Diagrams

The 5 Golden AP Rules for Drawing FBDs

  1. Single Dot: Represent the object as one dot (center of mass).
  2. Outward Arrows: Draw arrows starting ON the dot and pointing AWAY in the direction of each push or pull.
  3. External Forces Only: Include only real external forces acting ON the object (W, N, T, f, Fapp).
  4. No Forbidden Arrows: NEVER draw velocity (v), acceleration (a), or net force (Fnet) on an official FBD.
  5. Proportional Lengths: Equal opposite arrows mean balanced forces (a = 0). A longer arrow means acceleration in that direction.

Key Conceptual Reminders

  • Constant Velocity (a = 0): Forces are balanced! A puck sliding on frictionless ice needs NO forward force arrow.
  • Inclined Planes: Weight (W) always points straight vertically down. Normal Force (N) points perpendicular (90°) to the ramp and is always less than W.
  • Coupled Systems: Internal rope tension forms an equal and opposite Newton's 3rd Law pair, so it cancels inside the combined system FBD.
