📐 Grade 9 Constant Acceleration — Formula Sheet

1. Finding Acceleration a = (vf - vi) / t
2. Finding Final Velocity vf = vi + a · t
3. Displacement (from rest, vi = 0) d = (1/2) · a · t²
4. Displacement (average velocity) d = ((vi + vf) / 2) · t
💡 Key Problem-Solving Clues & SI Units:
  • "Starts from rest" → vi = 0 m/s
  • "Comes to a complete stop" → vf = 0 m/s
  • Velocity (vi, vf): m/s  |  Acceleration (a): m/s²  |  Displacement (d): m  |  Time (t): s
✅ The 3-Line Math Rule Example:
Line 1 (Formula):      vf = vi + a · t
Line 2 (Substitution): vf = 0 + 4 · 3
Line 3 (Answer):       vf = 12 (m/s)
